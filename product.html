The Ritual of Application

01. Mist

Spray into the air before you and walk through the mist to let the particles settle evenly.

02. Pulse

Apply directly to pulse points: wrists, behind ears, and the base of the throat for heat-driven diffusion.

03. Linger

Avoid rubbing the skin; let the essence dry naturally to preserve the integrity of the top notes.

04. Signature

Seeks a scent that is deeply personal, a whisper of self, not a shout.
